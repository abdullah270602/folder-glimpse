# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

FolderGlimpse contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful,
welcoming, and free from harassment. Be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing
experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention,
publishing another person's private information, or persistent disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **abdullahnaseem27@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and addressed in a way proportionate to the behavior, from a private warning
through temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Maintainers will avoid conflicts
of interest and protect reporters from retaliation.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, and public spaces
where someone is representing FolderGlimpse.
